About Me

I'm a second-year Computer Science student and AI Research Assistant at Vietnamese-German University - VGU . My current research focuses on few-shot learning, vision-language models, and real-time computer vision systems, aiming to develop more efficient and adaptive AI architectures.

My academic foundation is rooted in competitive programming, having earned a Third Prize in the Vietnamese Olympiad in Informatics - VOI during my time at Le Quy Don High School for the Gifted - Khanh Hoa. This experience instilled in me a deep love for algorithmic problem-solving and technical excellence.

In addition to my research, I lead Orzlab as a Content Creator. I am passionate about AI education and dedicated to making advanced concepts in mathematics and computer science accessible to everyone. Through Orzlab, I bridge the gap between complex research and public understanding through clear, visual demonstrations and educational content.
